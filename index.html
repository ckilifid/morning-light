Morning Light in Mustang Pens

Wild horses, gathered into roundup pens and painted in early light. Ellen was invited to join the roundups and photographed the herd — the horses coming in from the wild, circling the pen, then settling and finding their families. The paintings are where her fascination with light meets these animals in a working setting.

What I love to paint is light.Ellen
I just feel so lucky that I fell into this. I was always painting them running — the iconic freedom theme — but here it's something different.Ellen

Ellen's work

Ellen trained at Pratt. Her work took her to Africa, then back to the States, drawn to the West and the wildlife that call the Rocky Mountains home.

Her paintings have been carried by fine art galleries across the West.
